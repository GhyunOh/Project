## Big Data Project  
**Crime Data Analysis by Age, Gender, and Year (2012–2019)**

### Overview
This project analyzed crime data provided by the Korean National Police Agency, focusing on crime rates by type, age group, gender, and year (2012–2019).  
The main objective was to explore correlations, visualize trends, and gain insights into youth crime rates and motivations.

### Objectives
- Collect and preprocess crime rate data (by crime type, age, gender, and year).  
- Explore potential correlations between variables using statistical methods.  
- Visualize data through time-series plots, radar charts, and comparative graphs.  
- Investigate juvenile crime rates and motivations to identify social implications.  

### Data Description
- Source: Korean National Police Agency (CSV datasets).  
- Preprocessing: Basic cleaning in Excel, structured into a database using Python.  
- Variables: Crime type (major/minor categories), age group, gender, year (2012–2019).  

### Key Steps
1. Data Collection and Preprocessing  
   - Obtained CSV data from the police agency.  
   - Cleaned and structured data into a usable format.  

2. Correlation Analysis  
   - Checked correlations between crime type, age, gender.  
   - Found that many expected correlations were weaker than hypothesized.  

3. Visualization  
   - Produced graphs including gender-based comparisons, radar charts, and time-series trends.  
   - Highlighted juvenile crime trends and analyzed crime motivations.  

4. Redirection of Focus  
   - Initial assumption: strong correlations between crime type, age, and gender.  
   - Actual results: weak or no meaningful correlations.  
   - Adjusted focus toward **juvenile crime rates and motivations**, exploring whether real-world juvenile experiences align with recorded motivations in official crime data.  

### Results and Insights
- Gender- and age-based crime data from 2012–2019 showed expected distributions but lacked strong inter-variable correlations.  
- Juvenile crime rates and motivation analysis provided more meaningful insights into patterns of youth criminal behavior.  
- Project highlighted the **importance of refining data analysis methods** and the potential of applying machine learning/deep learning for future predictive work.  

### Limitations
- Time constraints and limited coding experience restricted deeper modeling.  
- Could not fully apply advanced methods like machine learning, which would have enhanced predictive capability.  

### Impact
- Offers a structured analysis of crime rates by demographic categories.  
- Provides groundwork for future studies on juvenile crime trends and motivations.  
- Demonstrates the need for advanced analytical techniques in social data projects.
